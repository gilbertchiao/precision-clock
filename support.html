支援

以下說明適用於 iPhone 版與 Android 版；兩個平台的按鈕位置與名稱可能略有不同。

如何切換一般模式與 HUD 模式？

啟動時畫面底部會顯示控制列，按「HUD」按鈕即可即時切換；也可以按齒輪按鈕，在設定頁切換。App 會記住上次選擇的模式。

如何進入全螢幕？

點一下畫面隱藏控制列與狀態列，只留下時鐘；再點一下即可叫出控制列。

HUD 模式怎麼使用？

開啟 HUD 模式後，時鐘會水平鏡像。將手機螢幕朝上平放，時間經擋風玻璃反射後即為正向。

請勿在駕駛中操作手機。使用前請將裝置妥善固定，不得妨礙視線或操作，並遵守當地交通法規。反射的可讀性受擋風玻璃與環境光影響，玻璃可能產生疊影。

毫秒準確嗎？

顯示的時間是每次螢幕更新時取樣的裝置系統時鐘。毫秒位數的更新間隔取決於螢幕更新率（例如 60Hz 或 120Hz），從讀取時鐘到畫面實際發光之間也有些微延遲。Precision Clock 不是量測或時間同步工具。

螢幕為什麼不會自動鎖定？

「保持螢幕恆亮」預設開啟，讓時鐘可以持續顯示。可在設定頁關閉；離開 App 後即恢復系統原本的自動鎖定行為。

離開 App 後亮度會改變嗎？

只有在設定頁開啟「調整亮度」時，App 才會改變螢幕亮度，並在你離開 App 時還原成進入前的亮度。

如何顯示日期或改用 12 小時制？

按齒輪按鈕開啟設定頁，在「時間與日期」區段可切換 24 小時制或 12 小時制（AM/PM），也可開啟日期顯示，並選擇日期在時間的上方或下方，以及日期格式。

直向時字比較小？

時間固定以單行顯示，因此直向的字級會小於橫向。需要最大字級時請將手機橫放。

Support

This help applies to both the iPhone and Android versions; button positions and names may differ slightly between platforms.

How do I switch between Normal and HUD mode?

A control bar is shown at the bottom on launch. Tap the "HUD" button to switch instantly, or tap the gear button and switch in Settings. The app remembers the mode you last used.

How do I go full screen?

Tap the screen to hide the control bar and status bar, leaving only the clock. Tap again to bring the controls back.

How do I use HUD mode?

HUD mode mirrors the clock horizontally. Lay the phone flat with the screen facing up and the time reads correctly when reflected in the windshield.

Do not operate your phone while driving. Secure the device so it does not obstruct your view or controls, and follow your local laws. Readability of a reflection depends on your windshield and ambient light, and the glass may produce a double image.

Are the milliseconds accurate?

The clock shows your device's system clock, sampled each time the screen refreshes. The millisecond digits update in steps that depend on your display's refresh rate (for example 60 Hz or 120 Hz), and there is a small delay between reading the clock and the screen lighting up. Precision Clock is not a measurement or time-synchronization instrument.

Why doesn't the screen lock automatically?

"Keep Screen Awake" is on by default so the clock stays visible. You can turn it off in Settings; normal auto-lock behavior returns when you leave the app.

Does the app change my brightness?

Only if you turn on "Adjust Brightness" in Settings. The app restores the brightness you had before when you leave it.

How do I show the date or use 12-hour time?

Tap the gear button to open Settings. In the "Time & Date" section you can switch between 24-hour and 12-hour (AM/PM) time, turn on the date, and choose whether it appears above or below the time and which date format to use.

Why is the text smaller in portrait?

The time is always shown on a single line, so the text is smaller in portrait than in landscape. Rotate the phone to landscape for the largest text.
