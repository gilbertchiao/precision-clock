Precision Clock 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 21 日
開發者：Gilbert Chiao

摘要

Precision Clock 不蒐集、不上傳、不分享，也不販售任何個人資料。

我們蒐集的資訊

無。本 App 不蒐集任何個人資料，也不需要帳號。

網路連線

本 App 不連接網際網路，不會從你的裝置傳送任何資料。

第三方 SDK

本 App 不包含任何第三方 SDK、分析、廣告或當機回報服務。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

本 App 會把你的偏好設定（文字顏色、字級、時間格式、日期顯示設定、HUD 模式、螢幕恆亮設定與亮度設定）儲存在裝置上 App 的本機儲存空間。這些資料不會離開你的裝置，我們也無法存取；刪除 App 時會一併移除。

使用的裝置功能

  • 螢幕亮度：若你開啟亮度選項，App 在使用期間會調整螢幕亮度，並在你離開 App 時還原。亮度數值不會被蒐集。
  • 閒置計時器：App 在前景時可避免螢幕自動鎖定。這是本機的顯示設定，不涉及任何資料蒐集。

追蹤

本 App 不追蹤你，不將你的資料用於廣告，也不與資料經紀商或其他公司分享資料。

兒童隱私

本 App 不向任何人（包含兒童）蒐集個人資料。

政策變更

我們可能不定期更新本政策。變更會發佈在本頁，並更新生效日期。

聯絡方式

若對本政策有任何疑問，請來信 precision-clock@sysop.tw。

Privacy Policy for Precision Clock

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Developer: Gilbert Chiao

Summary

Precision Clock does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect any personal data and does not require an account.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. It does not send any data from your device.

Third-party SDKs

The app does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (text color, font size, time format, date display settings, HUD mode, screen-awake setting, and brightness setting) in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is removed when you delete the app.

Device features used

  • Screen brightness: if you turn on the brightness option, the app adjusts your screen brightness while it is in use and restores it when you leave the app. Brightness values are not collected.
  • Idle timer: the app can keep the screen from auto-locking while it is in the foreground. This is a local display setting and involves no data collection.

Tracking

The app does not track you. It does not use your data for advertising, and it does not share data with data brokers or other companies.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact precision-clock@sysop.tw.
