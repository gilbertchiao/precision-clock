Precision Clock

在黑色畫面上以大字顯示目前時間，格式為 HH:mm:ss.SSS（預設 24 小時制，也可改為 12 小時制 AM/PM）。

功能

  • 大字時鐘，含毫秒；直向與橫向皆自動調整字級
  • 可選 24 小時制（預設）或 12 小時制（AM/PM）
  • 可選擇在時間上方或下方顯示日期，提供四種日期格式
  • HUD 模式：將時鐘（含日期）水平鏡像，經擋風玻璃反射後即可閱讀
  • 點一下畫面顯示或隱藏控制列，隱藏後即為沉浸式全螢幕時鐘
  • 四種文字顏色：白、綠、紅、琥珀；自動或手動字級
  • 可選擇保持螢幕恆亮與 App 內亮度調整，離開 App 時還原原本的亮度
  • 無需帳號、無廣告、無內購、不使用網路、不追蹤

iPhone：需要 iOS 17.0 以上。
Android：需要 Android 10 以上，即將在 Google Play 推出。

關於顯示的時間

顯示的時間是每次螢幕更新時取樣的裝置系統時鐘。毫秒位數的更新間隔取決於螢幕更新率（例如 60Hz 或 120Hz），從讀取時鐘到畫面實際發光之間也有些微延遲。Precision Clock 不是量測或時間同步工具。

行車安全

請勿在駕駛中操作手機。使用前請將裝置妥善固定，不得妨礙視線或操作，並遵守當地交通法規。反射的可讀性受擋風玻璃與環境光影響，玻璃可能產生疊影。

Precision Clock

Shows the current time as HH:mm:ss.SSS (24-hour by default, or 12-hour with AM/PM) in large, easy-to-read digits on a black screen.

Features

  • Large clock with milliseconds, sized automatically in portrait or landscape
  • 24-hour (default) or 12-hour time with AM/PM
  • Optional date above or below the time, in four date formats
  • HUD mode: mirrors the clock (including the date) horizontally so it can be read when reflected in a windshield
  • Tap the screen to show or hide the controls for an immersive full-screen clock
  • Four text colors: white, green, red, amber; automatic or manual font size
  • Optional screen-awake setting and in-app brightness; your original brightness is restored when you leave the app
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no network access, no tracking

iPhone: requires iOS 17.0 or later.
Android: requires Android 10 or later; coming soon to Google Play.

About the time shown

The clock shows your device's system clock, sampled each time the screen refreshes. The millisecond digits update in steps that depend on your display's refresh rate (for example 60 Hz or 120 Hz), and there is a small delay between reading the clock and the screen lighting up. Precision Clock is not a measurement or time-synchronization instrument.

Driving safety

Do not operate your phone while driving. Secure the device so it does not obstruct your view or controls, and follow your local laws. Readability of a reflection depends on your windshield and ambient light, and the glass may produce a double image.
